How I work with AI

I work with AI agents like Claude Code. The AI writes most of the code; I decide what gets built, in what order and when it's done right.

  1. I define the problem. What the system must solve, for whom and under which rules.

  2. I approve the design. Every part has a written plan before it's built.

  3. I build with tests. Automated tests that warn when something breaks.

  4. I review and deploy. I test in the browser, deploy over SSH and check that everything matches.
