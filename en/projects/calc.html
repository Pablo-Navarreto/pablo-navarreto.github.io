CALC

The tool Pablo's Serralheria uses to calculate and quote steel structures such as mezzanines, stairs and roofs. The job is laid out on a floor plan, seen in 3D, and out come the weight, the bars to buy, how to cut them and the price.

The problem

Quoting a structure by hand is slow, and getting it wrong costs money.

  • The steel weight had to be added up piece by piece.
  • Bars come in 6 meter lengths: cut them badly and the leftover goes to waste.
  • A quote by eye can be off by up to 40 %. One done piece by piece, around 10 %.

The solution

A tool in the browser where you draw the job and everything else comes out on its own, instantly.

  • Columns, beams, stairs and railings go on a floor plan, and the job shows up in 3D.
  • An algorithm decides how to cut the bars to waste as little as possible.
  • The price comes from the real weight, and the quote goes to FinanceIA in one click.

The 7 parts

Every job is a project, and inside it is everything needed to build it.

AssemblerThe job's floor plan: columns, truss beams, joists, stairs, railings and floor, with measurements and in 3D.
ProjectsEach job with its stage (quote, purchase, cutting, welding, painting, assembly), details, photos and videos.
Profile catalogTubes, U profiles and plates, with their weight per meter.
PDF readerReads the parts list from the engineer's project.
Cutting engineDecides how to cut each 6 meter bar to waste as little as possible.
Cutting sheetThe bars to buy and how to cut each one, as a PDF for the shop.
QuotePrice per kilo, per square meter or itemized, and it goes to FinanceIA.

The journey of a piece of data

What happens, step by step, from the floor plan to the quote.

  1. 1

    The job is laid out

    In the assembler, columns, beams, stairs and railings are placed on the plan.

  2. 2

    Pieces come out

    Each element becomes pieces with their profile, length and quantity.

  3. 3

    One formula

    Each piece's weight is its volume times the density of steel, 7,850 kg/m³.

  4. 4

    The cutting engine

    Pieces are sorted from longest to shortest and each goes into the first bar where it fits (First Fit Decreasing). It subtracts the saw blade width and splices any piece longer than a bar.

  5. 5

    Sheet for the shop

    Bars cut the same way are grouped: 8 bars like this, 6 bars like that.

  6. 6

    The price

    Steel, paint, welding wire, gas and cutting discs, and the final price.

  7. 7

    To FinanceIA

    In a single database transaction, with the quote number locked so it never repeats, the quote is created as a draft.

  8. 8

    A person signs off

    The quote stays a draft until Pablo reviews it and sends it to the client.

Security

It is an internal tool, but it stores clients' projects and photos.

PasswordsHashed, never as plain text. The same account as FinanceIA.
Login attemptsLimited per IP.
Each user's dataEvery query filters by user: nobody sees someone else's projects.
Database queriesPrepared statements, so nobody can inject malicious code.
Photos and videosOutside the public folder, per user and project.
KeysOnly on the server, outside Git.

Quality and delivery

  • The design follows the same interface guide as FinanceIA: both look the same.
